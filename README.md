A powerful, intuitive, and modern mobile accounting, billing, and invoicing application built with Flutter. **Payzo Books** empowers small-to-medium enterprises (SMEs), merchants, and freelancers to streamline end-to-end bookkeeping, generate professional tax invoices, monitor expenses, track inventory, and collect payments on the go.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

- **🧾 Professional Invoicing & Estimates**: Generate, customize, and issue compliant digital invoices and credit/debit notes with multiple itemized templates (`invoice-filled.svg`, `createnote.svg`).
- **📦 Inventory & Product Catalog Management**: Track inventory levels, stock valuation, SKUs, and product categories seamlessly (`Product_filled.svg`).
- **💳 Multi-Payment & POS Terminal Support**: Collect payments directly through integrated **Razorpay**, **PayPal**, and **Verifone** smart POS terminals (`Verifone.svg`, `razorpay.svg`).
- **📊 Real-Time Financial Dashboard**: Visual analytics for gross revenue, outstanding receivables, accounts payable, and net cash flow (`dashboard_purple.svg`).
- **📑 Expense & Bill Tracking**: Record vendor bills, categorize operational expenses with merchant receipts, and stay on top of recurring overheads (`billpayment.svg`).
- **✅ Approval Workflows & Status Tracking**: Multi-state document tracking (Draft, Pending Approval, Approved, Rejected, Paid) for enhanced team governance (`approved_box.svg`, `pendingbox.svg`).
- **🖨️ Instant Print, PDF & Document Export**: One-tap PDF generation, wireless thermal/office printing, and document sharing via messaging and email (`print.svg`, `export.svg`).

## 📲 Installation

To install the release build on an Android device:

1. Download the [`app-release.apk`](app-release.apk) file from this repository or the Releases section.
2. Enable **Install from Unknown Sources** in your Android device settings if prompted.
3. Open the APK file on your device and tap **Install**.
4. Launch **Payzo Books** and manage your business finances!

Alternatively, install via ADB:
```bash
adb install -r app-release.apk
```
